A notebook can hold an unfinished thought without asking what it will become. This space can do the same: a place to record an observation, work through a question, or remember a journey.

Some entries might explain a piece of physics. Others might be a few paragraphs about a book or a walk. They do not need to belong to the same category to be worth keeping.

## Start with something small

A useful note might begin with a question:

- What did I learn today?
- Which detail surprised me?
- What would I like to remember a year from now?

The answer need not be definitive. Writing it down gives it a shape that can be revisited.

> Leave enough room for the thought to change.

## Room to return

Over time, individual notes become an archive. Dates give them a little context; links connect them. The rest can stay simple.

A paragraph, a photograph, an explanation. Enough to begin.
